    Security & Privacy

    Change password

    At least 12 characters with letters, numbers and symbols. Changing it signs out every other session.

    Privacy policy

    The system stores personal data of students and faculty: registration/employee number, name, branch, year, role, and the dates and times of library visits.

    1. Purpose: the data is used only to record library attendance, manage occupancy, and prepare usage reports for the library and university.
    2. Minimum data: only the fields listed above are collected. No photos, phone numbers, addresses or biometrics.
    3. Access: only authorised library administrators may view logs, reports and backups. Reports shared with other staff must be for official library use.
    4. Sharing: data is not sold or given to outside parties. Third-party services in use are limited to the email provider (for sending reports) and Formspree (for the contact form, which carries only what is typed into it).
    5. Retention: visit logs are kept as long as the university needs them for reports; backups are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically; audit logs for 90 days.
    6. Security: access is protected by a password with lockout, encrypted connections where online, and an audit trail of admin actions.
    7. Downloaded reports: reports downloaded or emailed contain personal data. The person receiving them is responsible for storing them safely and deleting them when no longer needed. Reports should not be posted on public channels or shared on personal messaging apps.
    8. Rights and queries: members can ask the library administration to correct wrong details; wrong details are fixed by re-importing the member file.
    9. Breach: if the admin suspects unauthorised access, they should change the password immediately, review the audit log and inform the university IT team and the developer.
    10. Law: the university should confirm this policy meets India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its own data policy.
